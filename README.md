# KFK 2060 预言档案 | KFK 2060 Prediction Archive

### 一个自称来自 2060 年的神秘网友，在 2019 年留下了 282 条问答。站在 2026 年回头看，他究竟说中了多少？

2019 年，一位名叫 **KFK** 的网友出现在豆瓣，自称来自 2060 年，并回答了大量关于未来的问题。

他的回答涉及人工智能、科技、战争、经济、人口、房地产、社会制度，以及未来几十年人类生活的变化。这些内容曾在中文互联网上引发广泛讨论。

如今已经过去 7 年，那些关于未来的说法，有多少与现实吻合？又有多少已经被事实否定？

本项目整理了当年的 **282 条问答**，以 **2026 年 10 月**为核查基准，逐条对照现实事件与公开资料。它不试图证明 KFK 是不是穿越者，而是通过事实核查区分：

- 哪些预言已经实现？
- 哪些只是符合了部分趋势？
- 哪些已经被现实证伪？
- 哪些需要等到未来才能验证？
- 哪些内容本身就无法客观验证？

## 代表性结论 / Representative checks

- **已实现 / Fulfilled**：多数国家结婚率偏低、上证指数未达到 20,000 点，社会也没有实行国家分配结婚对象。
  Low marriage rates across many countries, the Shanghai Composite remaining below 20,000, and no state allocation of marriage partners.
- **部分实现 / Partially fulfilled**：全球不确定性与焦虑上升、房地产市场出现分化；特朗普 2020 年未连任，但在 2024 年再次当选。
  Global uncertainty and anxiety increased, housing markets diverged, and Trump lost re-election in 2020 but returned to office in 2024.
- **未实现 / Unfulfilled**：2047 年全球人口达到 80 亿的时间判断明显提前；2022—2023 年台海问题实现统一也未发生。
  The population milestone arrived much earlier than the predicted 2047 date, and no unification took place in 2022–2023.
- **待验证 / Pending**：2048 年以前的交通方式、2060 年的政治体制变化和房屋价值变化等长期判断。
  Long-term claims about transport before 2048 and political or housing changes by 2060 remain open.
- **无法验证 / Unverifiable**：发帖者身份、被删除的原问题，以及无法独立核实的叙事内容。
  The poster’s identity, deleted source questions, and claims without independent evidence cannot be verified.

## 网站功能 / Features

- 收录 282 条原始问答，以及原始回答、预言提炼和现实核查信息。
- 涵盖 AI、科技、经济、战争、人口、社会等 10 大主题。
- 支持关键词搜索、主题筛选、状态筛选和目标年份筛选。
- 支持列表与时间轴两种浏览方式。
- 为每条预言提供可追溯的现实依据与核查说明。

The archive includes 282 original Q&A entries, extracted claims, and real-world checks. It covers 10 broad topics and supports keyword search, topic and status filters, target-year filters, list view, and timeline view.

## 为什么做这个项目？ / Why this project?

我觉得，预言最有意思的地方，不是它听起来有多神奇，而是多年以后，我们终于可以拿着现实去对答案。

这个网站既是一次中文互联网考古，也是一次关于预测、巧合、趋势与事实的实验。它不要求读者相信或否定 KFK，只邀请读者回到原始问答和现实证据本身。

The interesting part of a prediction is not how mysterious it sounds, but whether reality can answer it years later. This project is both an archaeological record of Chinese internet culture and a small experiment in comparing predictions, coincidences, trends, and evidence.

## English Introduction

### A mysterious online user claiming to be from 2060 posted 282 answers in 2019. Seven years later, how many of them still hold up?

In 2019, a user named **KFK** appeared on Douban, a major Chinese social platform, claiming to be from the year 2060. KFK answered questions about AI, technology, geopolitics, the economy, population, housing, social systems, and everyday life in the decades ahead.

The original event attracted wide attention on the Chinese internet. Now, with seven years of real-world developments behind us, this archive revisits the claims one by one.

I collected the original **282 Q&A entries** and compared them with public events and available evidence as of **October 2026**. The goal is not to prove or disprove time travel. It is to distinguish specific claims from broad trends and to separate fulfilled, partially fulfilled, unfulfilled, pending, and unverifiable entries.

**Explore the archive:** [kfk2060.netlify.app](https://t.co/bsEZvE43UR)

The project is open source. Contributions, corrections, and additional evidence are welcome.
